Ronaldo Cohin (CEO) and Jade Autism were selected as part of the 2023 Meaningful Business 100 (MB100), an international recognition that celebrates leaders committed to combining profit and purpose. This aligns with the UN Global Goals to create a positive impact worldwide. Among various categories, we were chosen to represent 'Quality Education.'
